# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and considerate in communication
- welcoming different perspectives and experiences
- giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- personal attacks or insulting comments
- disruptive behavior during discussions or collaboration
- sharing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent bans from participation.

## Reporting

Please report unacceptable behavior by contacting the maintainers through a private and secure channel.
